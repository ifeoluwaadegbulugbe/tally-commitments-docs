# Tally Commitments for Jira

![Tally](assets/logo-144.png)

Every promise to a customer, kept. Tally finds customer commitments in Jira and Confluence, ties
them to the Jira work that delivers them, flags risk with plain rules, and drafts customer updates
that a person approves. Runs on Atlassian: no data leaves your site.
